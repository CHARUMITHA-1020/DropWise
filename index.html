About DropWise Smart Water OS

An end-to-end intelligent water monitoring and leak prevention architecture engineered for modern residential buildings, hostels, and housing communities.

System Architecture & Data Pipeline

How DropWise processes telemetry

  • Real-Time Sensor Simulator: Computes true differential volume balance ($\Delta V = (\text{Inflow} - \text{Outflow}) \times \Delta t$) every 2 seconds without cumulative time drift.
  • Three-Strike Leak Detection: Monitors continuous high-velocity flow ($\ge 15\text{ L/min}$). Only triggers after 3 consecutive abnormal cycles to prevent false positives from brief tap turns.
  • Cooldown-Protected Smart Alarms: Dedicated 10-second cooldown guards per alarm channel prevent notification fatigue while keeping underlying threshold sensors active.
  • Dual-Tier AI Intelligence: Combines Google Gemini cloud LLM inference with a zero-latency offline heuristic engine that functions even with no internet connection.

Future Physical Hardware Roadmap

Transitioning from prototype to physical deployment

To deploy DropWise in a real apartment or hostel building:

  • Controller: ESP32 Wi-Fi / Bluetooth IoT microcontroller with watchdog reset.
  • Tank Level: Waterproof ultrasonic transducer (JSN-SR04T) mounted on tank cap or submersible hydrostatic pressure sensor.
  • Flow Sensor: YF-S201 Hall-effect turbine flow sensor placed on the primary outlet manifold.
  • Pump Actuation: Optocoupler-isolated 30A solid-state relay module with manual mechanical override and dry-run safety sensor.
  • Telemetry Protocol: Lightweight MQTT broker with TLS encryption transmitting JSON payloads to the DropWise backend.

Critical Engineering Safety Notice

This web dashboard functions as a software visualization and simulation prototype. In physical installations, software alerts alone must never be relied upon as the sole safeguard against water tank overflows or pump burnouts. Real deployments require hardware float switches, an independent physical overflow pipe, and thermal motor circuit breakers.
